The footpath game

Walk 100 m without stepping onto the road. Tap or press space to jump. Hold the Duck button or the down arrow to duck. It is harder than it should be.

Check a real street

Every obstacle in the game is one you can tap on a real check. The walker, the kerb and the road are the same drawing the check makes of your street. Nothing here is uploaded or recorded; your best distance stays on your phone.

Right to Walk (righttowalk.life) is a Crashfree India prototype: the Footpath Check, Gurugram, method v1.0.
