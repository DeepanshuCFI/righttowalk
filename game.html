The footpath game

You walk 500 m of a Gurugram road: an afternoon stretch, one after dark, one in the rain. The footpath will block you; the only way past is the road, where the traffic is. Step down, step up, or wait for a gap. Pick who is walking. It is not a game you win. It is a walk you get through.

On a phone: tap the footpath to step up, tap the road to step down, or swipe up and down. Tap Wait to stop walking while traffic passes; tap again to continue. On a keyboard: arrows up and down, space to wait. Pause game freezes the scene.
