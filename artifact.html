Two minutes, three steps

1Where

One tap takes your location. Say which side of the road you are on.

2Tap what you see

Parked cars, a vendor cart, an open manhole, a dug-up stretch. Add one photo.

3The Court's test

Could you walk 100 m without stepping onto the road? You get the answer in the Court's words. You get a notice for the road's owner. Your check becomes a pin on the map.
