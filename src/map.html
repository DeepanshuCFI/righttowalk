Every pin is a check a citizen walked and a moderator read. Pins are rounded to about 10 m. "Verified" pins were re-walked by a trained volunteer. Pins that fail the 100 m test are stretches where a walker could not go 100 m on the footpath without stepping onto the road: the Supreme Court's own test for a footpath that exists (Maniyar Iliyaz, 19 June 2026, para 20(b)).

The record is open data (CC BY 4.0): checks.csv · checks.json · about the dataset. Map tiles from the OpenStreetMap community servers; loading them tells that server your IP address and the tiles you view, nothing else.

Right to Walk (righttowalk.life) is a Crashfree India prototype: the Footpath Check, Gurugram, method v1.0.
