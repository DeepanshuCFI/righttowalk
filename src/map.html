    Roads to walk are the roads on Haryana's published list. A check appears near a road when its start is within 60 m of an approximate line; this does not establish coverage of that road or either footpath side. Assignment sheet for volunteers.

    Each pin is a citizen observation read by a moderator. Locations are rounded to about 10 m. Quick checks describe one spot; only full checks can answer Right to Walk's 100 m continuity question. The distance is our method, not a threshold set by the Supreme Court. Read the method and limits.

    The record is open data (CC BY 4.0): checks.csv · checks.json · about the dataset. Map tiles from the OpenStreetMap community servers; loading them tells that server your IP address and the tiles you view, nothing else.
