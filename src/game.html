The footpath game

You walk 500 m of a Gurugram road. The footpath will block you; the only way past is the road, where the traffic is. Step down, step up, or wait. It is not a game you win; it is a walk you survive.

0 m
Court’s test, 100 m

 

On a phone: tap the footpath to step up, tap the road to step down, or swipe up and down. Hold Wait to stop. On a keyboard: arrows up and down, space to wait.

Check a real street

Every obstacle is one you can tap on a real check. Nothing here is uploaded or recorded. The backdrops are Gurugram for the feel of it; the obstacles are typical of the city, not a survey of any one spot.
