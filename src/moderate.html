Read, then publish

Every submitted check waits here. Confirm that the place exists, the answers fit the check mode, the photos match, and no person is identifiable before publishing. Reject spam or abuse and mark trials as test. For a linked re-walk, publish the second check first, then enter its full ID on the original record. Both checks must have coordinates on the same side, and the re-walk must start within 150 m. Record any disagreement in the private note before linking.

Moderators are listed in the moderators table; a sign-in link only works for those addresses. Sessions last an hour and renew while the page is open.
