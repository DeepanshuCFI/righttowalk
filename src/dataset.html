The Gurugram footpath record

Every published spot or stretch observation, as a file you can open in a spreadsheet or a script. Names are never in it. Locations are rounded to about 10 m. Nothing appears here until a moderator has read it.

Files

  • checks.csv: one row per published check, flat columns (below), escaped for spreadsheet use. Opens in Excel, Sheets, QGIS. Use JSON for exact raw text.
  • checks.json: the same rows with answers and problem counts as nested objects, plus the snapshot time.
  • stats.json: the headline counts and the by-sector and by-owner tables.
  • deaths.json: the deaths on record, pedestrians killed in Gurugram at places where a public record states what the road lacked, each with its sources and the rules of entry. Kept by hand, not by the nightly snapshot.

The repository schedules a daily snapshot at about 03:00 IST. The file date is not proof the job ran today. Check the workflow history if freshness matters. The map and report read the live record. Live JSON endpoints are public and read-only:

Columns

ColumnMeaning
idRandom identifier of the check (UUID). The first eight characters are the reference shown to the walker.
created_atWhen the check was sent in (UTC, ISO 8601).
modequick (one spot) or full (a longer check with ten scored questions and a separate 100 m continuity answer). The mode alone does not establish distance walked.
methodMethod tag. Older footpath-check-v1.2 records and newer footpath-check-v1.3 records may coexist; the later tag separates quick spot passability from full 100 m continuity.
lat, lonWhere the check started, rounded to four decimals (about 10 m). Empty when the walker named the road instead.
length_mStraight-line distance between start and end GPS fixes, metres; not the distance walked along the footpath. Empty if no reliable end fix exists.
sideleft or right: the footpath on the walker's left or right as they walked.
road, sectorAs typed and chosen by the walker. Free text; not geocoded.
landWhat the walker reports beside the stretch: homes, shops, city or sensitive (school, hospital, bus stop or market). This is context, not a legal determination of the applicable width.
road_classDerived from four questions: arterial, subarterial, collector, local.
ownerThe agency the walker believes maintains the road: gmda, mcg, hsvp, dtcp, nhai, pwd, unknown. This is self-reported until checked against official road records.
spot_passableQuick checks only. yes means the walker could pass this one spot without entering traffic; no means they could not. It is not a 100 m result. Legacy quick answers have been reclassified from the old gate field.
gateFull checks only: Right to Walk's 100 m continuity question. yes means the walker reported staying on the footpath for 100 m; no means they could not. The Supreme Court did not specify this distance. Empty means the question was not answered.
i1 to i10The ten items of a full check: footpath present, clear of obstructions, a real crossing, slow enough to cross, wide enough for two, ramps and tactile tiles, level surface, lit at night, kerb height and continuity, buffer and shade. Values yes, no, partly (items 2, 3, 7), cant (item 8). When i1 is no, items 2, 5, 6, 7, 9 and 10 fail automatically.
score, bandFull checks only. Score 0 to 100 over the items the walker could judge; band g walkable, a walkable with repairs, r not walkable. A failed 100 m answer sets r. These are project labels, not legal compliance or crash-risk estimates.
anchorFull checks: would you let a 10-year-old or your grandmother walk this alone, 0 (definitely) to 4 (never). Reported, never scored.
obs_*How many times each problem type was tapped: obs_parked, obs_vendor, obs_shop, obs_ramp, obs_electric, obs_green, obs_manhole, obs_slab, obs_dug, obs_waste, obs_furniture, obs_water, obs_riding, obs_cattle, obs_shrine.
photo_countPhotos actually published with the pin after moderation, not merely selected on the phone. Photos are not embedded in the files.
verified, verified_at, verification_check_idA linked later published check supports a moderator's re-walk verification. The date and linked check identify that evidence; true alone does not mean the entire road was measured.
legacy_verifiedOlder records marked verified without a linked second check. The old mark is preserved for transparency but does not count as linked verification.

Some of these are stored privately for moderation and record integrity; see the consent explanation before submitting. Photos remain private until publication. If a photo cannot be safely re-encoded without camera metadata, it is not uploaded.
