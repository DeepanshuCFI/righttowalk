The Gurugram footpath record

Every published check, as a file you can open in a spreadsheet or a script. Names are never in it. Locations are rounded to about 10 m. Nothing appears here until a moderator has read it.

Files

  • checks.csv: one row per published check, flat columns (below). Opens in Excel, Sheets, QGIS.
  • checks.json: the same rows with answers and problem counts as nested objects, plus the snapshot time.
  • stats.json: the headline counts and the by-sector and by-owner tables.
  • deaths.json: the deaths on record, pedestrians killed in Gurugram at places where a public record states what the road lacked, each with its sources and the rules of entry. Kept by hand, not by the nightly snapshot.

Snapshots are taken once a day at about 03:00 IST by a GitHub Action in the public repository DeepanshuCFI/righttowalk; the map and report read the live record. The live JSON endpoints are public and read-only:

Columns

ColumnMeaning
idRandom identifier of the check (UUID). The first eight characters are the reference shown to the walker.
created_atWhen the check was sent in (UTC, ISO 8601).
modequick (one spot, about two minutes) or full (a stretch of about 500 m, eleven questions).
methodMethod tag, footpath-check-v1.2: method v1.0 weights with problem types recorded.
lat, lonWhere the check started, rounded to four decimals (about 10 m). Empty when the walker named the road instead.
length_mStraight-line distance between the start and end fixes of a full check, metres; empty for a quick check or when no end fix was taken.
sideleft or right: the footpath on the walker's left or right as they walked.
road, sectorAs typed and chosen by the walker. Free text; not geocoded.
landWhat sits beside the stretch: homes, shops, city (city commercial street), sensitive (school, hospital, bus stop or market within the stretch). Sets the IRC:103-2022 walking-zone width that applies.
road_classDerived from four questions: arterial, subarterial, collector, local.
ownerThe agency the walker believes maintains the road: gmda, mcg, hsvp, dtcp, nhai, pwd, unknown. A guess; verified re-walks correct it.
gateThe Court's test. yes: the walker could go 100 m on this side without stepping onto the road; no: could not; empty: not answered (quick checks may skip it).
i1 to i10The ten items of a full check: footpath present, clear of obstructions, a real crossing, slow enough to cross, wide enough for two, ramps and tactile tiles, level surface, lit at night, kerb height and continuity, buffer and shade. Values yes, no, partly (items 2, 3, 7), cant (item 8). When i1 is no, items 2, 5, 6, 7, 9 and 10 fail automatically.
score, bandFull checks only. Score 0 to 100 over the items the walker could judge; band g walkable, a walkable with repairs, r not walkable. A failed gate is always r.
anchorFull checks: would you let a 10-year-old or your grandmother walk this alone, 0 (definitely) to 4 (never). Reported, never scored.
obs_*How many times each problem type was tapped: obs_parked, obs_vendor, obs_shop, obs_ramp, obs_electric, obs_green, obs_manhole, obs_slab, obs_dug, obs_waste, obs_furniture, obs_water, obs_riding, obs_cattle, obs_shrine.
photo_countPhotos the walker sent. Photos are published with the pin on the map, not in these files.
verifiedtrue when a trained volunteer re-walked the stretch and a moderator confirmed the record.
