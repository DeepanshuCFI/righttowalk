# Right to Walk source and claim ledger

Last reviewed: 30 October 2026. This ledger records what a public claim may say; it does not establish that a standard applies to every road. Recheck the source and date before formal outreach.

| Claim or method | Source and status | Public wording rule |
|---|---|---|
| Walking and demarcated, maintained footpaths | [*Maniyar Iliyaz v. P. Ayyappan*, 2026 INSC 647, para 20(b)](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2024/42514/42514_2024_3_1501_71888_Judgement_19-Jun-2026.pdf). Signed judgment. | Attribute the duty to the Court; retain the exact paragraph reference. |
| Footpath audit categories and the specified fifty cities and national highways | [*S. Rajaseekaran v. Union of India*, 7 October 2025, paras 35.1–35.5](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2012/20376/20376_2012_8_1503_64828_FinalOrder_07-Oct-2025.pdf). Signed order. | Say which authorities and places the direction covers. Gurugram is outside the specified fifty-city list. |
| 100 m continuous walk | Right to Walk operational rule, method version in the record. No 100 m threshold found in the three signed judgments/orders reviewed. | Call it **our 100 m continuity check**. Never call it the Court's test or a statutory standard. A quick check of one spot cannot answer it. |
| Weighted score and bands | Right to Walk exploratory index; weights chosen by this project. Field reliability and predictive validity are untested. | Show item answers and method version. Do not call a score a legal-compliance finding or crash-risk estimate. |
| Route length | Optional start/end coordinates yield straight-line separation, not distance walked. | Say "distance not measured" where unknown; never substitute 500 m. Label any GPS separation as such. |
| IRC:103-2022 | [IRC publication catalogue](https://www.irc.nic.in/WriteReadData/LINKS/Catalogue%20Updated%2009%20July%2C%20%202025%20%281%292aaca7ba-f12e-4931-8fd7-39dfb20199f6.pdf) lists a published second revision. [*Rajaseekaran*, paras 17–19](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2012/20376/20376_2012_8_1503_64828_FinalOrder_07-Oct-2025.pdf) reproduces specific 2022 width and kerb figures and discusses application. The remaining final clauses have not been checked for this project. | Court-reproduced figures may be cited with their land-use context, but the citizen form does not measure compliance. Do not present other numbers from an earlier draft as final 2022 thresholds. Check the published clause and road owner before a formal allegation. |
| Accessible roads | [Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016, s.41(1)(c)](https://upload.indiacode.nic.in/showfile?actid=AC_DL_64_796_00001_201649_1547618167086&filename=a2016-49_act.pdf&type=actfile); [2021 Harmonised Guidelines](https://depwd.gov.in/en/document/harmonised-guidelines-standards-for-universal-accessibility-in-india-2021/). | Have disabled walkers review the questions and observations; distinguish a visible obstacle from a measured standards breach. |
| State rule powers | Motor Vehicles Act 1988, [ss.138(1A), 210D](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/indiacode/handle/123456789/1798?col=123456789%2F1362&view_type=search); [*Rajaseekaran*, 7 October 2025](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2012/20376/20376_2012_8_1503_64828_FinalOrder_07-Oct-2025.pdf). | Say the powers exist. Haryana's notified-rule status requires a current Gazette/RTI check. |
| Road ownership and the 100-road challenge | Current frame combines a press notice, a news list and approximate OpenStreetMap lines. Official identifiers, extents and ownership are incomplete. | Treat a nearby observation as proximity only. Do not infer that a whole road was walked or failed. Obtain official road records before an agency or Court comparison. |
| W.P.(C) 844/2026 pilot-town reporting | Signed October 2026 order and later listing outcome not located in this review. | Keep reported directions out of public claims until the signed order and current docket are verified. |

Update this ledger whenever the method, legal sources or source records change. Preserve the date and reason for each revision in Git history.
